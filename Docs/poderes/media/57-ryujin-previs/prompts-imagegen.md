## Conceito 1
+
Use case: stylized-concept. Create a professional game VFX concept sheet for a Minecraft Bleach mod, not a gameplay screenshot. A coherent 3 by 4 grid of eleven numbered panels and one legend panel, dark slate background, refined voxel art characters and pixelated orange-gold FLAME PARTICLES, burning embers and smoke. No translucent flat magical sheets or colored glass. Same blocky shinigami in black robes and white captain coat, katana, across every panel, three-quarter camera, clear readable body poses and generous spacing. Header "RYUJIN — ESTUDO VISUAL", subheader "Conceito, não gameplay". Each panel contains one main action pose with faint ghost poses and directional arrows, effects distinct and not obscuring the character. Panel labels exact: "01 Selo Bankai": flame streams converging onto sword, brief lowered horizontal blackened katana held pointing OUTWARD away from torso, then small ghost of neutral recovery stance, no permanent pose. "02 Corte frontal": sealed silver sword descending physical cut, no fire. "03 Varredura": sealed silver sword horizontal physical sweep, no fire. "04 Rajada": downward cut launches a narrow traveling ground flame wave. "05 Leque": horizontal swing sweeps real orange flame particles from player right to player left, wide arc. "06 Tornado": rotating sword invokes two vertical flame helices with a safe clear center. "07 Jokaku": sword lowers and points, upright tongues of fire rise as a circular enclosing dome around enemies away from caster. "08 Investida": forward dash with sword extended and narrow fiery wake. "09 Higashi": coiled torso releasing a decisive close sword cut, concentrated white-yellow glowing sword edge, short tight hot flame burst and sparks at contact, NOT a ranged beam. "10 Minami": sword planted at floor, six dark ash skeletons emerging through small fire vents. "11 Corte de Vapor": overhead preparation then decisive cut sending long narrow bright flame path with smoke at sides. Last panel: flame swatches and the three pose phases drawn as small silhouettes. All eleven actions shown, no ice, no Hyorinmaru. Crisp careful anatomy of blocky figures and sword pointing outside body. These are artistic targets only, no UI damage numbers.

## Conceito 2
+
Use case: stylized-concept. A polished storyboard for Higashi attack in a Minecraft Bleach mod. Wide composition with three equal consecutive frames, caption "HIGASHI — PREPARAR / CORTAR / RECUPERAR". Same blocky shinigami wearing black robes and open white captain haori, blackened katana with a fine glowing gold cutting edge, rocky voxel training courtyard at dusk. First frame: foot anchored, sword pulled outside right hip, shoulders and torso coil, minimal small embers, katana never intersects torso. Second frame: hips and shoulders rotate into forceful short-range diagonal slash, body weight shifts to front foot, blade points outward into blocky enemy immediately ahead, white-hot edge flash, explosive cluster of short orange and yellow pixelated flame particles and hot sparks at CONTACT. Distinct arc of tiny burning particles, no flat translucent slash ribbon, no long beam. Third frame: follow-through outside body then return towards normal relaxed combat stance, fading embers and a little smoke, no permanently held special pose. Arrows track the sword and hip rotations, clean matte technical concept art with voxel proportions and strong lighting. Small footer "Proposta visual · não é captura do jogo". High-quality professional animation direction board, readable actions. No ice, no magical pink ribbons, no extra arms.
